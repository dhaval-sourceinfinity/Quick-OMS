  • Quick OMS is built for small and growing businesses managing daily orders — including wholesalers, distributors, FMCG suppliers and importers who need orders, stock, customers and invoices handled in one place.

  • For manual and B2B order-first workflows, yes. Quick OMS is an order-first OMS for small and growing businesses that need orders, stock, customers, invoices and dispatch together — without the broader ERP complexity of Odoo or the ecommerce-only focus of Shopify.

  • Enterprise plans support custom workflows, multi-branch operations, higher invoice volumes, bespoke API integrations and dedicated onboarding support tailored to how your business runs.
